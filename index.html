Got Questions?

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about using Travault for your adventures.

How does Universal Capture work with Instagram reels or links?

Simply tap 'Share' on any Instagram Reel, YouTube short, or travel web link and select Travault (or paste the link into the app's Universal Capture bar). Our smart extraction engine automatically identifies the location, coordinates, relevant tags, and saves it into your Vault in seconds.

Does the app truly work without internet or mobile network?

Yes! Travault is engineered local-first using embedded Hive caching. Once you save places or build an itinerary, all data — including notes, route checkpoints, packing lists, and coordinates — remains accessible without an active cellular or Wi-Fi connection.

Can I plan group road trips and share expenses with friends?

Yes. You can invite friends to any trip using a simple join link or code. All members can collaboratively contribute spots to the daily schedule, log fuel expenses, check off packing items, and chat within the trip room.

Is Travault free to use?

Travault is completely free to download and use on Android via Google Play. There are no intrusive advertisements or paid paywalls blocking core trip planning features.

Can I export my travel itinerary as a PDF?

Yes. With one tap, you can export clean, beautifully formatted day-by-day itineraries and checklists into a PDF to share with family or keep handy on your device.
