FAQs

Common questions

How do I save travel spots?

Tap the save icon on any place in the app to add it to your vault. You can organize spots into lists like "Paris trip" or "Bucket list" to keep everything tidy.

Is Travault free?

Yes. Travault is free to download and use on Android. We may offer optional premium features in the future.

Can I use Travault offline?

Core parts of your vault are available offline so you can view saved places and trip details when you don't have a connection.
