Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 31, 2025

Thank you for using Travault, a mobile application designed to help you discover, save, and plan travel spots. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal info: Name, email, profile image, and travel preferences you provide.
  • User content: Saved spots, notes, custom tags, images you upload, and planned travel dates.
  • Usage data: Device type, IP address, session activity, feature usage, and crash reports to improve the app.
  • Location data: With your permission, we use your location to suggest nearby places and improve recommendations.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To power app features and securely save your travel data across devices.
  • To personalize your experience with relevant content and suggestions.
  • To monitor performance, fix bugs, and develop new features.
  • To send important service updates and, if you opt in, marketing communications.

3. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We may share information with trusted service providers (e.g. cloud hosting) under confidentiality agreements, or when required by law.

4. Your Choices

  • You may access, update, or delete your personal data in the app settings.
  • You may opt out of marketing emails via the unsubscribe link in any email.
  • You may revoke location permissions at any time in your device settings.

5. Security

We use industry best practices including secure cloud storage, encryption, and regular security reviews to protect your data.

6. Children's Privacy

Travault is not intended for users under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If we become aware of such data, we will delete it.

7. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services (e.g. Google Maps, Firebase) that have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them.

8. Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. Significant changes will be communicated via in-app notifications or email.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at:

Email: travaultapp@gmail.com
