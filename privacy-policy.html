Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 31, 2025

Thank you for using Travault, a mobile application designed to help you discover, save, and plan travel spots. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Info: Name, email, profile image, and travel preferences you provide.
  • User Content: Saved spots, notes, custom tags, images you upload, and planned travel dates.
  • Usage Data: Device type, IP address, session activity, feature usage, and crash reports to help us improve the app.
  • Location Data: With your explicit permission, we access your location to suggest nearby places and provide smart recommendations.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To power core app features and securely save your travel data across devices.
  • To personalize your experience with tailored suggestions and a relevant content feed.
  • To monitor and analyze performance, fix bugs, and develop new features.
  • To send important service updates and, if you opt-in, marketing communications.

3. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We may share information with trusted service providers (like cloud hosting) who are bound by confidentiality agreements, or if we are legally required to do so by law.

4. Your Choices

  • You may access, update, or delete your personal data from within the app's settings.
  • You may opt out of marketing emails by clicking the "unsubscribe" link in any email.
  • You may revoke location permissions at any time through your device's settings.

5. Security

We are committed to protecting your data. We use industry best practices including secure cloud storage, end-to-end data encryption, and regular security reviews to safeguard your information.

6. Children’s Privacy

Travault is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under this age. If we become aware that we have, we will take steps to delete such information.

7. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party tools like Google Maps for location services or Firebase for backend infrastructure. These services have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them.

8. Updates to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Significant changes will be communicated to you via in-app notifications or by email.
